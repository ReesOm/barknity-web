Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

The short version. Barknity helps dog owners near each other meet up. To do that we store what you put on your profile, roughly where you are, and the messages you send. We never show your exact position to anyone unless you choose to share it with one specific match, and that sharing stops after two hours. The app is free and paid for by advertising: some ads come from Google AdMob, and some are promotions we arrange directly with local businesses. We do not sell your data. You can download everything we hold, or delete your account and it is really deleted.

Who is responsible for your data

Barknity is run by Mohammed Zekri, based in Spain. Under the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation we are the "data controller" for the information described here.

For anything in this policy, including requests to see or delete your data, contact barknity@gmail.com.

Who can use Barknity

Barknity is for adults only. You must be 18 or over. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up and refuse accounts under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has created an account, we delete it.

Location

This is the most sensitive thing the app handles, so we are specific about it.

  • Your general area is stored so we can show you people and walks nearby. Other users never see your coordinates — only a rough distance, such as "3 km away".
  • Live sharing is separate, and always your choice. You can share your position with one specific match at a time. It lasts a maximum of two hours, you can stop it whenever you like, and it stops on its own if you close the app. It is never broadcast, never shown to strangers, and never on by default.
  • Walks have a meeting point you choose. Anyone who can see the walk can see that point — that is the purpose of it.

Things collected automatically

  • A push notification token, so we can send notifications to your device. You can turn notification categories off in Settings.
  • Basic technical logs — request times and error messages — kept briefly so we can find faults.
  • Crash and error reports from the app and our servers, sent to Sentry. They describe what went wrong and on what kind of device. We configure Sentry not to receive your name, email, IP address or the content of your messages.
  • The country you are in, worked out from the town or position you give us, and the date you were last active. We use these to count how many people use Barknity in each country, never to show anything about you to other users.
  • How many swipes you have used in the current period, and whether you have already been shown or rewarded for an ad in that period, so the swipe limit and ad frequency work correctly.
  • When you sign up, a security check by Cloudflare Turnstile that confirms you are a person and not an automated script.

Advertising

Barknity is free to use and is paid for by advertising. There are two kinds.

  • Google AdMob ads. While you swipe, the app may show a full-screen ad, and when you reach the swipe limit you can choose to watch a short video in exchange for extra swipes. These ads are selected and served by Google. To do that, Google's software in the app collects your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which gives a rough idea of location), how you interact with the ads, and technical information about the app's performance. Google uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. When you watch a video for extra swipes, we also pass Google a random account number (not your name or email) so that Google can confirm to us that the video was completed.
  • Promotions from local businesses. We sometimes show a promotion when you open the app, for example from a vet, groomer or pet shop. These are chosen by us, not by Google, based only on the country or area you are in. We record which promotions you have seen and when, so you are not shown the same one repeatedly. We do not give the business any information about you.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before Google can use your data for personalised ads. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (on most Android phones: Settings → Google → Ads). To change your answer at any time, open the app and go to Profile → Settings → Ad privacy choices.

How Google uses data from apps that show its ads is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for or store your sexual orientation, religion, political views, health, ethnicity, or anything else GDPR treats as a special category. We do not track you across other apps or websites ourselves, and we never give advertisers your profile, your photos, your messages or your precise location.

How long we keep it

  • While your account exists, we keep your profile and messages so the app works.
  • Live location shares expire after at most two hours and are then deleted.
  • Crash reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days.
  • Data collected by Google for ads is kept under Google's own retention rules, which we do not control.
  • When you delete your account, your profile details, photos, dog, swipes, matches and location history are deleted immediately, and your photo files are removed from storage. The messages you sent to other people have their content erased, but the fact that a conversation existed remains on the other person's side — we cannot delete their record of what happened to them.
  • Reports and blocks are kept after deletion, because they exist to protect the person who made them.

Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or object to us using it. You can withdraw consent for location sharing at any time by stopping the share, and consent for personalised ads as described under Advertising.

Two of these are built into the app and need no request at all:

  • Download your data: Profile → Settings → Download my data.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Settings → Delete my account. There is also a web form if you have already uninstalled the app.

For anything else, email barknity@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to your national data protection authority. In Spain that is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in a form we can read. Sessions expire and refresh tokens rotate. Photos are checked to make sure they are genuinely images before they are stored.

No service is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting your data, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything significant we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
